# Masar — Team Meetings & Synchronisation Guide

> **"A meeting without an agenda is a discussion; a meeting without documented decisions and owners is a waste of time."**

This directory contains records of all official team syncs, sprint kickoffs, slice reviews, and supervisor updates for the Masar graduation project.

---

## 1. Meeting Rhythm & Cadence

Per [08 — Plan & Timeline](../docs/08-plan-and-timeline.md#8-working-agreements):

| Cadence | Event | Purpose | Duration | Attendees |
|---|---|---|:-:|---|
| **Twice weekly** (Sun & Wed) | **Async Written Standup** | Post in team channel: **Done / Next / Blocked**. | — | All 8 members |
| **Weekly** (Saturday) | **Team Sync & Demo** | Walk through completed PRs on staging; plan upcoming week. | 45 min | All 8 members |
| **End of Slice** (Every 2–3 wks) | **Slice Review** | Demonstrate the slice's "Done when" statement live. | 30 min | All 8 members |
| **Fortnightly** | **Supervisor Update** | Report progress, showcase staging demo, consult on blockers. | 30 min | Abdelrahman + Lead reps with Dr. Hend |
| **Monthly** | **Risk Register Review** | Audit top risks in [10 — Risks & Assumptions](../docs/10-risks-and-assumptions.md). | 20 min | All 8 members |

## 2. The 24-Hour Blocking Rule

> **CRITICAL RULE:** If you are blocked on code, dependencies, environments, or specification ambiguity for **more than 24 hours**, you MUST escalate directly to **Abdelrahman Megahed**.  
> Silent blocking is the number one cause of project failure in graduation teams.

---

## 3. Asynchronous Standup Format

Post this format directly to the team communication channel on **Sunday morning** and **Wednesday morning**:

```markdown
**Standup — [Your Name] — [Date]**
1. **Done:** What issues / PRs were merged or completed since last update.
2. **Next:** What specific issue / task from the current slice is being worked on next.
3. **Blocked:** Any blockers, questions, or external dependencies (or "None").
```
